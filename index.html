What to validate before choosing
This is the diligence checklist that turns directional research into a decision.
Talent supply and cost
Role-by-role hiring volume, compensation, attrition, notice periods and time-to-fill.
Office and operating cost
Micro-market rent, fit-out, commute, utilities and occupancy assumptions.
Policy eligibility
Current government policy, exact eligibility criteria, application windows and approval process.
Execution ecosystem
Relevant provider case studies, leadership hiring, legal setup and local operating readiness.
City profiles combine source-supported sector associations with explicit diligence questions. They do not claim to be an audited cost ranking or a substitute for a role-specific market study.
Understand / Policy & incentives

Policy intelligence without the fine-print blind spot.

Policy information is only useful when the source, scope, eligibility and current status are clear. Start with a state policy and verify every provision before relying on it.
Important: this is an intelligence and diligence tool, not legal or tax advice. Policies can change, incentives may be conditional, and the existence of a scheme does not mean a specific company qualifies. Verify with the relevant government department and qualified advisors before using any incentive in a business case.
